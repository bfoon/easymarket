2. Scope

This Notice explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data of buyers, sellers, visitors, and support contacts who use EasyMarket.

3. Data We Collect

Account & Profile:

name, username, password (hashed), email, phone, photo, store details (for sellers).

Orders & Logistics:

items purchased/sold, prices, delivery addresses, preferred pickup location, geo code identifiers, tracking numbers, return/refund records.

Payments:

payment method selection and status, transaction IDs and results from payment processors. We do not store full card numbers or mobile wallet PINs.

Communications:

messages and chats between buyers/sellers, support requests, email/SMS/WhatsApp communications.

Reviews & User Content:

ratings, comments, images you upload.

Device & Usage:

IP address, device identifiers, app version, browser, language, cookies, pages viewed, referral URLs, approximate location inferred from IP or user input.

Security & Compliance:

fraud signals, failed logins, token verification logs (including IP and activity), audit logs.

Social Sign-in:

if you connect Google or others, we receive profile basics per your permissions.

4. Sources of Data

Directly from you; automatically from your device; from sellers or buyers you interact with; from payment, logistics, analytics, and anti-fraud partners; and from lawful public sources.

5. How We Use Data

  • Provide and improve our marketplace, process orders, payments, shipping, returns, and customer support.
  • Personalize content, recommendations, and promotions; run referrals, promo codes, auctions.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse; secure accounts; enforce terms and policies.
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax, accounting, law-enforcement requests).
  • Communicate about your account, orders, and—with consent or as allowed—marketing.
Legal bases (where applicable): performance of contract, legitimate interests (running a safe marketplace, preventing fraud, improving services), consent (e.g., marketing), legal obligation.

6. Sharing Your Data

We share data with:

  • Sellers/Stores you purchase from (only what's needed to fulfill your order).
  • Logistics providers (e.g., Easy Move) for delivery and returns.
  • Payment processors (e.g., Wave, Qmoney, Afrimoney, Gamswitch) to process payments and refunds.
  • Service providers (hosting, analytics, communications, customer support) under contract.
  • Other users (when you post reviews or chat).
  • Law enforcement or regulators where required by law or to protect rights.
  • Business transfers (merger, acquisition, asset sale) subject to this Notice.
We do not sell your personal data.
